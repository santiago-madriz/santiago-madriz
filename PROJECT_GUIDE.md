# Project Guide

This guide explains how the public repositories fit together and keeps the portfolio intentionally small at first glance.

## Recommended review order

1. **Playwright Quality Engineering** — flagship production-style test architecture.
2. **AI-Assisted QA** — responsible AI boundaries applied to quality work.
3. **Cypress Quality Framework** — framework migration and comparable coverage.
4. **Playwright QA Workshop** — mentoring and communication through executable examples.
5. **Spanish Sentiment Lab** — Python/API quality, security, and modernization.
6. **Holocron Quiz** — product engineering with failure-mode and accessibility tests.

## Scope and privacy

Examples use public demo applications or Santiago's own photography site. The repositories do not reproduce proprietary employer code, customer data, internal selectors, private architecture, or confidential incidents.
